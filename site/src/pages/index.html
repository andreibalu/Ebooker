ii. Your library

MP3M4BAAC

Bring your own files.

Import MP3, M4B and AAC audiobooks from Files, iCloud Drive or any provider in the Files app. A book split across many files arrives as one book, its tracks in order.

DRM-protected files, such as Audible and Apple Books purchases, can’t be played.

Your own server, in your pocket.

If you run Audiobookshelf, or someone shares a server with you, connect it in Shelves and stream your library. Unpaged talks to your server directly. Nothing passes through anyone else.

iv. Moments

Save the line, not just the time.

Tap once to bookmark a moment. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, Unpaged listens to the passage and names it for you: a title, a short note, the key quote and its mood. The quote is checked against the transcript, so it’s what the narrator actually said.

Back after a week away? Where Was I? writes a short recap of the story so far.

All of it runs on your iPhone. Your audio and transcripts aren’t sent anywhere.

Plain bookmarks are free on every iPhone. Naming and recaps need Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iOS 26) and Unpaged Plus.

viii. Questions

Good to know.

More answers on the support page.

Is Unpaged really free?

Yes. Playback, your own books, the LibriVox catalog, Audiobookshelf, bookmarks, CarPlay and the equalizer are free, with no ads. Unpaged Plus is optional and adds AI moment naming, recaps and iCloud Sync.

Which files can I play?

MP3, M4B and AAC, imported from Files, iCloud Drive or any provider connected to the Files app. DRM-protected files, such as Audible .aax or Apple Books purchases, aren’t supported.

Does it work offline?

Anything you’ve downloaded plays offline, including LibriVox books. Streaming books, including those from your Audiobookshelf server, and browsing the LibriVox catalog need a connection.

Which iPhones get the AI features?

They use Apple Intelligence, so you need an iPhone 15 Pro or Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other iPhones the AI buttons are hidden and everything else works normally.

Can I reach my Audiobookshelf server away from home?

Yes, if the server has an https:// address. A plain http:// address only works on your local network; put the server behind HTTPS for remote access.

How do I restore purchases on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then open Settings → Unpaged Plus → Restore Purchases.
