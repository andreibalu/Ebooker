## Description

Unpaged is an independent audiobook player for iPhone, built only with Apple frameworks. It plays the M4B and MP3 audiobooks people already own, streams or downloads from their own Audiobookshelf server, and offers 20,000 free LibriVox classics. No account, no ads, no tracking.

This update makes iCloud Sync free for everyone. Library, progress, moments, recaps and listening history follow the listener across their iPhones through their private iCloud account. Listeners can also keep the audio of books they import in iCloud Drive, so a new iPhone gets the whole book back.

On-device Apple Intelligence gets more reliable: moments name themselves with a title, quote and mood, and story-so-far recaps welcome listeners back to a book. Both now handle far more passages of real fiction, and nothing leaves the device.

It builds on 1.5: moment search, an End of chapter sleep timer, Continue Listening widgets, and Audiobookshelf offline downloads with progress sync.

## Helpful details

Unpaged is made by one developer, Andrei Baluta, and links no third-party code. Playback, iCloud Sync, LibriVox, Audiobookshelf, widgets and CarPlay are free. An optional Unpaged Plus subscription covers the Apple Intelligence features on supported iPhones. The app supports Dynamic Type at accessibility sizes, Bold Text, Button Shapes, Display Zoom and VoiceOver.
