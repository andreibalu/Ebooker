## Description

Unpaged is a clean, private audiobook player for iPhone. Play the M4B and MP3 audiobooks you already own, stream or download from your own Audiobookshelf server, or pick from 20,000 free classics. No account, no ads, no tracking.

YOUR BOOKS, YOUR LIBRARY
Import M4B, MP3, and AAC files from Files or iCloud Drive. Chapters stay in order and multi-file audiobooks stay grouped. Rename books, favorite them, and pick up exactly where you left off.

YOUR AUDIOBOOKSHELF SERVER, IN YOUR POCKET
Sign in to your Audiobookshelf server with your username and password or an API key. Stream your library or download books for offline listening, browse by series, collections, authors, and narrators, and keep browsing your cached library offline. Listening progress syncs back to the server. Your login stays in the iPhone Keychain.

FREE CLASSICS ON YOUR SHELVES
Browse more than 20,000 public-domain LibriVox audiobooks. Stream instantly, download for offline listening, compare alternate recordings, and hear a short sample before you choose a narrator.

FREE iCLOUD SYNC
Keep your library, progress, moments, recaps, and listening history in sync across your iPhones through your private iCloud account. If you like, Unpaged also stores the audio of the books you import in iCloud Drive, so another iPhone, or this one after a reinstall, can download them. Stored audio counts toward your iCloud storage. Free classics and Audiobookshelf books download again from their source.

MADE FOR DAILY LISTENING
• Background playback and lock-screen controls
• Continue Listening widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen
• CarPlay with hands-free library search
• Siri shortcut for your latest book
• Playback speed from 0.8× to 2×
• Sleep timer, including End of chapter
• 5-band equalizer, saved per book
• Adjustable skip and smart resume
• Reading activity and listening stats
• Moments: bookmarks for the passages you want to keep, searchable across every book

UNPAGED PLUS
One optional subscription, monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial, for on-device Apple Intelligence:
• Moments that name themselves: a title, quote, and mood for each bookmark
• Story-so-far recaps, including a welcome-back recap when you return to a book after a few days
AI features run entirely on your iPhone and need a device that supports Apple Intelligence. Earlier AI unlock purchases keep working.

FREE TO START
Playback, iCloud Sync, moments, widgets, CarPlay, the equalizer, stats, LibriVox, and Audiobookshelf are free. Unpaged runs on any iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No analytics or tracking. Unpaged has no server of its own: sync goes through your private iCloud account, and your audio leaves the iPhone only if you choose to store it in iCloud.

LibriVox recordings are made by volunteers. Credit and thanks to them.
