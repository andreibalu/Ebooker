# Unpaged flow coverage

The native suite drives the real SwiftUI app on the dedicated `Unpaged e2e` simulator with the tester-army/e2e mobile engine. A DEBUG launch with `-e2e-fixture` opens a separate SwiftData store seeded with two books of generated PCM audio, two moments, one reading session and 14 LibriVox catalog rows. Relaunch steps omit `-e2e-reset-fixture`, so a value that survives the relaunch was saved to disk.

This matrix covers every iOS feature except Apple Intelligence and CloudKit itself, which have their own qualification. The iCloud screens, the sync gate and audio in iCloud run against fakes (`icloud.e2e.ts`). Three local fakes replace outside services:

- `support/fake-librivox.ts` serves the LibriVox feed and chapter audio on localhost.
- `support/fake-audiobookshelf.ts` serves an Audiobookshelf server on localhost.
- The local `Products.storekit` configuration stands in for the App Store.

## Validation, 2026-10-18 (rating prompt)

`tests/rating-prompt.e2e.ts` (9 cases) covers the prompt's wiring: a book played to its end, a recorded finished book, the listening habit, and the cases that must stay quiet (first-day install, onboarding, a presented Settings sheet or Plus paywall, playing audio, a stream still loading, thin listening) plus the once-per-version limit across a relaunch. The decision rules have their own isolated tests (`RatingPromptPolicyTests`, written from a failure list at the top of that file). All 9 cases passed on five dedicated simulators: iPhone 16 Pro (iOS 18.0), iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.5), iPhone SE 3rd generation (iOS 26.5), iPhone 18 Pro (iOS 27.0) and iPhone 18 Pro Max (iOS 27.0). Requests are counted by the DEBUG `-e2e-rating-stub` label, not Apple's sheet. A manual launch without the stub raised the real "Enjoying Unpaged?" sheet on iOS 18.0, the SE and the Pro Max. A tenth case, added in review, starts a LibriVox stream through "play latest book" with the player closed while the fake feed holds the audio for seven seconds. It fails on the PR head (the prompt asked during the load) and passes with `isPreparingPlayback` counted as busy, on iPhone 18 Pro (iOS 27.0) and iPhone SE 3rd generation (iOS 18.0). Rerun with `E2E_SIMULATOR_UDID=<udid> E2E_BUILD_DIR=<build> npx e2e run tests/rating-prompt.e2e.ts` from `e2e/`.

Known limits: the "played to its end" case retries on a fresh launch (up to eight times), because on loaded simulators AVPlayer sometimes runs past the end of the fixture's silent WAV and never finishes the book. That was seen before this change and was not checked on `main`. Nothing asserts what Apple does with the request (its own yearly limit, or showing nothing), and no device run was made.

## Validation, 2026-10-14 (large text header)

`tests/accessibility-layout.e2e.ts` sets the simulator to accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large text with Button Shapes and Bold Text, then asserts the "My Library" header title keeps a usable width, one row of height and a place in the top 20% of the screen, the three tabs stay short rows, each of the four header buttons opens its screen, and the Shelves "Collections" heading stays on one line. It restores `large` text and turns both switches off in `afterEach`, even on failure. The app relaunches, so no simulator reboot was needed. On `main` (before `66d8e9b`) it fails because the title's width is 0; on the fix it passes. At `98b5937` on the dedicated simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27.0) it ran with `moment-search`, `library`, `native-flows`, `purchases` and `free-books`: 44 passed, 0 failed, in 12 minutes. `audiobookshelf` passed 28 of 28 at `28d4ca9`. Rerun with `E2E_SIMULATOR_UDID=<udid> E2E_BUILD_DIR=<build> npx e2e run tests/accessibility-layout.e2e.ts` from `e2e/`. Frames come from `boundingBox()`. The assertions use loose bounds, not exact sizes, and the test covers Moment Search, Plus, Settings and Import through their sheets, not the Files picker.

## Validation, 2026-10-12 (release 1.5.0)

Full native suite at `b02fdc5` on the same simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27.0): 108 passed and `live-catalog.e2e.ts` skipped (opt-in), in 37 minutes. Rerun from `e2e/` with `E2E_SIMULATOR_UDID=<udid> E2E_BUILD_DIR=<build> npx e2e run`.

Six code-review rounds on the release branch added these cases, most of them shown failing before their fix:

- Cached Audiobookshelf shelves keep the offline banner when an overlapping load succeeds online (`cached shelves keep the offline banner…`).
- A queued position is never sent to the server as a different user (`ABS durable progress never sends a queued position as a different user…`).
- A local position saved before `absProgressWrittenAt` existed counts as of its last playback, so an older server position does not replace it (`ABS durable progress keeps a legacy local position played after the server position`).
- Play Latest Book resumes an Audiobookshelf book from its saved marker, after reconciling with the server (`Play Latest Book resumes an ABS book from its saved marker…`).
- Moment search disables rows for a disconnected Audiobookshelf server and for a missing track file (`moment-search.e2e.ts`).
- A cached recap whose anchor is within 60 s of the saved position survives a relaunch (`welcome-back.e2e.ts`).
- Play after an End of chapter expiry at a file end continues with the next file (`chapter sleep stops at a track end, and Play continues with the next file`).

The last fix, `d3008fb`, changed only the legacy-progress fallback; `audiobookshelf.e2e.ts` passed on it apart from one failure in `a server book is added…` (the Remove dialog did not appear), which passed 3 of 3 reruns. The round-6 fix was checked with `player.e2e.ts` (11 of 11) and the native-flows, library, widget and moment-search files: 29 of 30 passed. The failure, `offline Shelves keeps cached books…`, stopped scrolling at the top of the chart; that code is unchanged, and the test passed 1 of 2 reruns. No E2E drives the Continue Listening widget refresh after an iCloud orphan merge or a mid-session CloudKit import.

After merge, a seventh review round made the end-of-file advance depend on the item the sleep timer stopped, not on the playback position. `offline Shelves keeps cached books…` now checks the first chart row instead of scrolling to a specific book, and passed 4 of 4. `player.e2e.ts` and `native-flows.e2e.ts` passed 25 of 25. With the shared scheme set to serial testing, all 295 Swift tests and 8 UI tests pass.

## Validation, 2026-10-11 (wave 1 features)

Native suite on the dedicated `Unpaged e2e integration` simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27.0) at `555d580`, 103 tests in 14 files. The full run passed 102, with `live-catalog.e2e.ts` skipped because it is opt-in. The run before it, at `a358666`, passed 100 of 101. Its one failure was a test fault: `local playback advances chapters and creates a persisted manual moment` tapped the moment sheet's Done button before SwiftUI enabled it, and failed about one run in three. The test now waits for Done to be enabled, and passed 5 of 5 on its own.

A code review of the merged branch found three app bugs, fixed before the final run:

- A queued offline progress report overwrote a newer position that another device had sent to the server. Covered by `ABS durable progress drops a queued stale position after newer server progress`.
- Resuming after adopting a newer server position reported the old local position back to the server. Covered by `ABS durable progress resumes from a newer server position five minutes ahead`.
- Cancelling an Audiobookshelf download could delete the manifest of a download restarted during the cancel. No E2E test drives that race.

Manual checks on the same simulator, 2026-10-12:

- **Continue Listening widget.** The small and medium widgets were added on the Home Screen through SpringBoard's widget gallery and checked in light and dark appearance. They show the empty state ("Start a book in Unpaged") when no snapshot exists. Tapping the widget cold-launches into the player at the saved position. The medium Play button resumes a paused book. The check found the cover overflowing into the widget margin and cropping the text. It now renders as a square cover clipped after framing. The circular and rectangular widgets were added through Lock Screen Customize. They show the book with matching progress (24 percent on the ring, 25% on the bar), and tapping the rectangular widget cold-launches the app into playback. While a book is loaded, iOS shows Now Playing in their place.
- **App Group in signed profiles.** A Release build signed with automatic provisioning lists `group.andreibaludev.Pageless` in the entitlements of both `Pageless.app` and the `DownloadActivity` extension, and in both team provisioning profiles. A local `archive` and an `-exportArchive` with `method: app-store-connect` (not uploaded) signed both bundles with Apple Distribution and the "iOS Team Store" profiles (no device list). Both profiles and both signed binaries list the group, with `beta-reports-active` set.
- **Welcome-back recap with the real model.** The Plus trial was bought through the local StoreKit sheet, with local AI on and the book last played 4 days ago. On the first try no card appeared, as designed for a failure: this simulator has no on-device speech recognition asset, the SpeechAnalyzer path fails, and the recap never requests speech permission for the fallback. With `-e2e-transcript-file` supplying the narrated chapter's text, the on-device model returned a headline and a correct recap in under 5 seconds, and the saved recap reappeared after a relaunch without the hook. Real transcription still needs a device.

The final full run (`01a11326-a97b-74b1-bcfd-fba8a8cd70f8`) passed all 67. The run before it (`01a11309-db66-7f6f-bb7e-c26929b3043b`) passed 65 of 67, and both failures were test faults, fixed before the final run:

- The Today's Pick helper counted one day short between midnight and 1 a.m. while daylight saving time is in effect.

Earlier full runs that day found and fixed these test faults:

- A Plus trial left over from a purchase test run on its own. `purchases.e2e.ts` now clears the StoreKit ledger after each test as well.

- Locators that matched a node twice while a removed card animated out.

The suite also found three app bugs, fixed in the same change:

- Deleting the book loaded in the player left the mini player and Now Playing showing the deleted book.
- Deleting a downloaded book while it was still preparing to play did not cancel the load. A Codex review found this one, and a unit test covers it.
- The downloads link, `unpaged://library/downloads`, opened Library underneath a pushed detail screen instead of showing the download.

## Not covered by the native suite

- Apple Intelligence smart save and recap, CloudKit carrying records between devices, the iCloud Drive daemon moving real audio, and the restore match. Retained isolated tests cover AI fallback/cancellation, recap invalidation, CloudKit schema constraints, restoration confirmation and file/model rollback; they do not qualify these complete native journeys.
- CarPlay, Control Center, lock screen metadata and background audio. These need a physical device.
- The system Files picker. The import hook replaces only the picker.
- Real App Store and Audiobookshelf accounts.

## Isolation and safety

- Use only the dedicated `Unpaged e2e` simulator. The config refuses any other.
- `-e2e-reset-fixture` deletes only `Application Support/E2EFixtures`, the saved scene state, the fixture's own Audiobookshelf Keychain item and the preference keys onboarding writes.
- In fixture mode the app keeps its Audiobookshelf login under a separate Keychain service, so tests never touch a real login.
- `purchases.e2e.ts` buys only against `Products.storekit` and clears the local StoreKit ledger before and after each test.
- `onboarding.e2e.ts` resets and grants microphone and speech permissions for the app on the dedicated simulator only.
- The fake servers listen on 127.0.0.1 and close after each test.
