Unpaged is a calm, focused audiobook player. Bring your own books, stream free classics from LibriVox, or connect your own Audiobookshelf server. No account, no ads, no tracking.

YOUR OWN AUDIOBOOKS
• Import MP3, M4B, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, Opus and WAV files from your phone or any storage provider
• Chapters are read from M4B and MP3 files automatically
• Unpaged copies the audio into its own storage and never touches your originals

20,000+ FREE CLASSICS
• Browse the full LibriVox catalog of public-domain audiobooks
• Stream instantly, or download a book to listen offline
• Hear a 20-second sample before you add a book
• Hand-picked collections and a daily pick to get you started

AUDIOBOOKSHELF
• Connect your self-hosted Audiobookshelf server over your home network or VPN
• Browse and search your libraries, then stream any book
• Your listening progress is sent back to your server

MOMENTS
• Save a moment with one tap when a passage stays with you
• Add a name and a note, then jump straight back to it later

LISTENING THE WAY YOU LIKE
• Variable playback speed and adjustable skip intervals
• Sleep timer
• A five-band equalizer with presets, saved per book
• Resumes a few seconds back so you never lose your place
• Lock screen, notification and Bluetooth headphone controls

ANDROID AUTO
• Browse your favorites, library and free books from your car screen
• Jump between chapters and save moments while you drive
• "Play Latest Book" shortcut and voice search

READING ACTIVITY
• A listening heatmap, streaks and your best listening days
• Totals for time listened and books finished

ON-DEVICE AI (SUPPORTED PHONES)
• On phones with Gemini Nano, Unpaged can name a saved moment from the passage you just heard and write a recap of the story so far
• Everything runs on your phone. Your audio and text are never sent to a server. The speech model downloads only after you agree.
• AI features are off by default, and the rest of the app works the same on every phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no ads
• Your library stays on your phone, and Android backup can restore it on a new phone
• Unpaged has no server of its own

Unpaged is also available on iPhone.
